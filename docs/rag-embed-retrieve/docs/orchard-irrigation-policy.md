# Orchard Irrigation and Frost Policy (synthetic)

The north block apple orchard is watered by drip lines, with one emitter per tree delivering four litres per hour. Irrigation runs before dawn to limit evaporation and to keep leaves dry, which reduces scab.

Soil moisture probes at 30 and 60 centimetres decide whether a block is watered. A block is skipped when the 60 centimetre probe reads above 28 percent volumetric water content.

Frost protection uses overhead sprinklers, not the drip system. Sprinklers start when the wet-bulb temperature at canopy height drops to 1 degree Celsius and keep running until the sun is up and ice on the buds has visibly melted.

Water drawn under the district allocation is logged daily. The orchard may not exceed 900 cubic metres per week between June and September.
